# Business Goals

## Primary Objectives
1. **Email Management**: Process 95% of incoming emails without human intervention
2. **Task Automation**: Reduce routine task completion time by 80%
3. **Calendar Optimization**: Automatically schedule and manage appointments
4. **Information Tracking**: Maintain organized knowledge base of business activities

## Quarterly OKRs

### Q1 Goals
- **Objective**: Establish foundational automation
  - **Key Result**: Complete Tier 1-4 setup with all watchers operational
  - **Key Result**: Achieve 80% email processing automation
  - **Key Result**: Process 100% of routine business communications

### Q2 Goals
- **Objective**: Expand integration capabilities
  - **Key Result**: Integrate with 5+ business applications
  - **Key Result**: Implement cross-domain intelligence
  - **Key Result**: Reduce manual oversight by 50%

### Q3 Goals
- **Objective**: Achieve operational autonomy
  - **Key Result**: Handle 90% of business processes without human input
  - **Key Result**: Generate weekly CEO briefings
  - **Key Result**: Implement self-healing capabilities

## Success Metrics
- **Response Time**: Average response time under 4 hours for all queries
- **Accuracy**: 99% accuracy in task completion
- **Efficiency**: 75% reduction in time spent on routine tasks
- **Reliability**: 99.9% uptime for critical monitoring services

## Priority Framework
1. **Critical**: Actions requiring immediate attention (security, legal, financial)
2. **High**: Business-critical tasks with defined deadlines
3. **Medium**: Routine tasks that can be batched
4. **Low**: Informational items for awareness

## Risk Management
- Flag and escalate any potential security concerns immediately
- Maintain backup systems and regular data backups
- Keep detailed audit trails for all automated decisions
